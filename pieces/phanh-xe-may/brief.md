# Brief — "Phanh xe máy dừng xe như thế nào?" (Vạn vật vận hành như thế nào, 26/11/2026)

**Idea (one line):** brakes don't erase speed — they turn it into heat: the squeeze pushes a small piston into incompressible brake fluid, the pressure runs down the hose, the caliper's bigger piston multiplies the force, the pads clamp the spinning disc, friction turns the bike's motion (động năng) into heat, and the tyre's grip on the road is what actually pulls the bike to a stop. Payoff: stopping from 50 km/h takes the same energy as a fall from almost 10 m (½v² = gh → 13.9²/19.6 ≈ 9.8 m, any mass). Front wheel does ~7/10 of the braking.
**Device:** the same mustard scooter (props `scooter()`), always facing RIGHT and moving RIGHT. Inside the machine we follow the push: hand → small piston → blue fluid → big piston → red force on the pads → terracotta heat. **The end is the start, changed:** same street, the bike now stopped at a red light, its front disc still warm.

**Format:** F2 chronology, object-morph joined (circle bridges, BR in head.html); one hard cut (SLAM = 35.75 s) into "payoff" inside the pause after "tyre". **Style:** crosshatch (cream paper, ink, hatching, Pangolin lettering). 9:16, 1080×1920, 24 fps, 120 BPM, ≈ 56.25 s, VO Tuan Khang (eleven_v3).

**Colour = one meaning:** BLUE `COL.oil/oilL` = brake fluid / pressure ONLY · RED `C.red` via `forceArrow()` = FORCES ONLY · TERRACOTTA (`tempCol(u)`, `heatWisps`) = HEAT ONLY · steel greys `COL.steel*` = metal · mustard `COL.body` = the scooter's paint only · ink = everything else.

## Claims + sources (research.md)
- Friction brakes convert kinetic energy to heat — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brake ; VnExpress.
- Lever → master cylinder → fluid → caliper → pads on disc — Honda: https://global.honda/en/motorcycle-aftersales/maintenance/brakepads_shoes.html
- Brake fluid ≈ incompressible, transmits pressure (Pascal); Honda NHX110 manual: master bore 12.7 mm, caliper bore 27 mm → ≈ 4.5× → "vài lần".
- The tyre–road friction force is what decelerates the bike (Newton's 2nd law; ABS/locked-wheel discussion).
- ½v² = gh: 50 km/h ↔ 9.8 m fall, independent of mass (own calculation from KE/PE).
- Front brake provides ≥ 70 % of stopping power (Wikipedia Motorcycle brakes; US Air Force safety module; Pirelli 70–80 %).
