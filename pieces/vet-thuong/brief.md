# Brief — "Vết thương tự lành ra sao?" (Vạn vật vận hành như thế nào, 23/11/2026)

**Idea (one line):** the scab is NOT new skin — it is dried clot, a lid; the real repair happens underneath: the vessel squeezes and platelets plug the gap, fibrin nets the blood into a clot that dries into a scab, white cells eat germs (red/swollen/warm), fibroblasts weave collagen and new vessels grow in to fill the hole, and skin cells crawl in from both edges UNDER the scab until they meet — then the scab is pushed off. Moist (clean, covered) wounds heal faster than dry, crusted ones; the scar is remodelled for a year and reaches only ~80% of the old strength.
**Device:** the hero is ONE SKIN CELL (`heroCell` in props.js: a peach paper rounded-square with a face) living at the LEFT edge of the cut; its partner is the cell at the RIGHT edge (same peach + small teal hair-clip, `partner: true`). They start apart, separated by the wound; the payoff is them meeting. **The end is the start, changed:** the same knee as the hook; the scab falls off, a pink patch of new skin underneath, the hero waves from it.

**Format:** F5 fixed-hero journey, morph-joined on the hero; one hard cut (SLAM ≈ 37.25 s) into "meet" inside the pause after "crawl". **Style:** cut-paper — KHUNG HÌNH GỌN (flat fills, light grain, no dotted patterns, ≤ 12 small objects per frame, each r ≥ 18; ≤ 1 small-particle effect per scene). 9:16, 1080×1920, 24 fps, 120 BPM, ≈ 61.5 s, Vietnamese voice-over (Tuan Khang, eleven_v3).

**Colour = one meaning:** see the COL header in `src/props.js` (PEACH skin cells · RED blood · YELLOW platelets · CREAM fibrin · BROWN scab · LAVENDER white cells · GREEN germs · ORANGE inflammation · TEAL collagen/fibroblasts · BLUE moisture).

## Claims + sources (research.md)
- Vessels constrict at once; platelets plug, fibrin mesh → clot (PMC7536089; MSD Manual). Scab = dried clot (Cleveland Clinic).
- Neutrophils within hours, macrophages from ~2 days; redness/warmth from widened vessels, swelling from leaky vessels (PMC5087220).
- Fibroblasts lay collagen, new vessels, granulation tissue ~day 4 (PMC5087220, PMC4352699).
- Keratinocytes migrate from the edges UNDER the scab and help detach it (Raja 2007, Cleveland Clinic).
- Moist wounds heal faster than dry/scabbed (AAD; Winter 1962) — script says "thường lành nhanh hơn".
- Remodelling ≥ 1 year; scar ≤ ~80% of the original strength (PMC4352699, PMC5087220) — script says "chừng tám mươi phần trăm".
