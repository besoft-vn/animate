# Brief — "Cầu vồng xuất hiện ra sao?" (Vạn vật vận hành như thế nào, 27/11/2026)

**Idea (one line):** a rainbow is not an object at a place: sunlight from BEHIND you enters raindrops in front of you, bends (violet most, red least), bounces off the back of each drop and comes back out at ~42° (red) / ~40° (violet) from the line of the sunlight; each drop sends your eye ONE colour; all the drops at that angle around the shadow of your head form a ring — seen from above it is a full circle; and everyone sees their own.
**Device:** the hero (terracotta doodle) as the viewer. **The end is the start, changed:** the hook's back view (hero walking toward a rainbow's foot that slides away) → at the end the hero stands still next to a friend, and each has their OWN rainbow centred on their own head's shadow.

**Format:** F5 fixed-hero journey, morph-joined on the hero; one hard cut (SLAM = 51.5 s) into "payoff" inside the pause after "circle". **Style:** sketchbook (plain page, graphite + ink, scribble colour, Pangolin lettering). 9:16, 1080×1920, 24 fps, 120 BPM, 65.25 s, Vietnamese VO (Tuan Khang, eleven_v3).

**Colour = one meaning:** TERRACOTTA = hero only · SPECTRUM `SPEC` (red, orange, yellow, green, blue, violet) = colours OF LIGHT only (rays leaving a drop, rainbow bands, strands inside a sunbeam) · PALE YELLOW `COL.sun/sunD/beam` = white sunlight / the sun only · GREY-BLUE `COL.drop/dropL` = water only (drops, rain) · graphite = ground, clouds, angle arcs, guide lines, arrows · ink = outlines, lettering. Friend in the end scene: graphite/ink doodle (NOT terracotta).

## PHYSICS RULES (the 6b reviewer checks these — get them right)
- **Rainbow order:** RED on the OUTSIDE (top), VIOLET on the INSIDE. `rainbowArc` already does this — never reverse.
- **Sun BEHIND the viewer, rain IN FRONT.** Side views: sun low on the LEFT, sunlight travels LEFT→RIGHT; returning coloured light travels RIGHT→LEFT and DOWN to the eye. Back views: sun behind the camera; the hero's shadow points AWAY from us (up the screen); the rainbow is centred on the head's shadow (ANTI).
- **In the drop:** light enters at the upper-left, bends, reflects ONCE off the back (right side), leaves from the bottom going down-left. Use `dropTrace()` (verified numerically) — don't hand-draw paths. Violet bends MORE than red. The SPEC spread is EXAGGERATED ~5× for visibility: add a small graphite note "(vẽ phóng đại)" where the fan is shown.
- **Angles:** measured between the returning ray and the line of the sunlight continued (a dashed `guideLine`). Red ≈ 42°, violet ≈ 40°. Red returns STEEPER, so the eye gets red from HIGHER drops, violet from LOWER drops.
- **Each drop sends the eye ONE colour** (the others miss the eye — show them going past, faint).
- Never say/draw "7 colours". Never put the rainbow at a fixed place you can walk to.

## Claims + sources (research.md)
- Red outside ~42°, violet inside ~40° (WMO Cloud Atlas; NWS Flagstaff; HyperPhysics).
- Sun behind, < 42° altitude; centre = antisolar point = shadow of your head (NWS, Met Office).
- Refraction in, one internal reflection, refraction out; violet bends more (n 1.331 red vs ~1.342 violet; OpenStax).
- Each drop sends the eye one colour; each person sees a different rainbow (ASU course notes).
- Full circle, the ground hides the rest; seen whole from aircraft (Met Office, NASA APOD 30/09/2014, USGS).
- Rainbow is a continuous spectrum; "7 colours" is Newton's convention (Met Office) — we never say "7".
