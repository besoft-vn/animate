# Brief — "Vì sao bầu trời màu xanh?" (làm lại 16/11/2026, theo góp ý "nhiều hạt nhìn rối mắt")

**Idea:** sunlight is all colours mixed; tiny air molecules knock short (blue) waves in every direction, so blue reaches your eye from the whole sky. **Device:** the sketchbook hero (terracotta doodle) follows sunlight from the sun into the air and back to the eye. **End = start, changed:** the hook's sky page again, now at sunset (orange), the hero understanding.

**Style:** sketchbook (graphite + ink on a paper page, one accent per meaning). **Format:** F5 fixed-hero journey, morph-joined, one slam (hard cut) into the payoff "dome". 9:16, 24 fps, 120 BPM grid, 58.75 s, voice Tuan Khang (eleven_v3).

## KHUNG HÌNH GỌN — the rule for this re-do (the user found the first version too busy with particles)
- One subject per frame, lots of bare paper. Never fields of dots, specks, stars, motes, bubbles.
- "Many molecules" = **at most 8–10**, each **r ≥ 22** (on 1080 wide), well spaced. Blue scatter arrows: at most 8.
- At most ONE small particle-type effect per scene (e.g. a few sparkle ticks), only where the eye should be.
- No full-frame textures/hatching fields; shade only the subject (`litHatch`), backgrounds stay page.
- Check every frame at phone size (still at --scale 0.33) and ask "is anything speckled or busy?" — if yes, remove.

## Colour = one meaning
terracotta = the hero only · yellow (`COL.sun`) = the sun/sunlight · `COL.blue`/`COL.blueL` = blue light and the blue sky · `COL.red`/`COL.redL` = red light · `COL.violet` only in "violet" · `COL.orange` only at sunset/end · everything else graphite & ink.

S. Smith) · horizon air mass ≈ 38–40× zenith (Kasten & Young) → "hàng chục lần".
