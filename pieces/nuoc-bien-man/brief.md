# Brief — "Vì sao nước biển mặn?" (Vạn vật vận hành như thế nào, 25/11/2026)

**Idea (one line):** rain takes up a little CO2 and turns slightly acidic; it slowly weathers rock on land and dissolves its minerals; rivers carry them to the sea (too dilute to taste — sea ≈ 300× saltier than river water); undersea volcanoes and hot vents add more; the sun evaporates only the WATER — the salt stays; the loop has run for hundreds of millions of years: all the sea's salt spread on land would be a layer > 160 m thick (USGS); salt also settles out (sediments, shells), so salinity holds at ≈ 3.5 %.
**Device:** the hero "Giọt" (sketchbook terracotta doodle, `heroGiot`) rides the water cycle and carries ONE plum salt grain (its badge) from the rock to the sea, and LEAVES it there when it evaporates. **The end is the start, changed:** same beach; now the hero tastes the sea and a thought bubble shows the mountain rock.

**Format:** F5 fixed-hero journey, morph-joined on the hero; one hard cut (SLAM = 38.75 s) into "payoff" inside the pause after "time". **Style:** sketchbook (plain page, graphite + ink, scribble colour, Pangolin lettering). 9:16, 1080×1920, 24 fps, 120 BPM, ≈ 58.25 s, Vietnamese VO (Tuan Khang, eleven_v3).

**Colour = one meaning:** TERRACOTTA = hero only · BLUE `COL.water/waterL` = water (rain, river, sea) · PLUM `COL.salt/saltL` = SALT / dissolved minerals (salt grains, the hero's badge, the salt layer) · GREEN `COL.acid/acidL` = CO2 / acid only · YELLOW `COL.sun/sunD` = sunlight / evaporation only · RED-ORANGE `COL.hot/hotL` = volcanic / vent heat only · graphite = rock, arrows, ticks · ink = outlines.

## Claims + sources (research.md)
- Rain absorbs CO2 → slightly acidic (carbonic acid) → weathers rock, releases ions → rivers carry them to the sea (USGS, NOAA).
- Rivers ≈ 0.12 g/L dissolved solids vs sea ≈ 34.5–35 g/kg → sea ≈ 300× saltier (research.md).
- Hydrothermal vents / undersea volcanoes add minerals (NOAA, USGS). Chloride mostly from volcanic gases over Earth's history (White, Geochemistry) — the script says only "góp thêm khoáng chất".
- Evaporation removes water, salts stay (USGS).
- Salt spread over land → layer > 500 ft ≈ 166 m ≈ 40-storey building (USGS) → script "hơn một trăm sáu mươi mét".
- Salt removed via sediments / organisms; near steady state ≥ 100 My (Britannica); salinity ≈ 35 ‰ = 3.5 % (NOAA).
- The end says "một phần trong đó từng là đá núi" (sodium from weathered rock), NOT "all".
