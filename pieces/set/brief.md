# Brief — "Sét hình thành như thế nào?" (Vạn vật vận hành như thế nào, 22/11/2026)

**Idea (one line):** in a thunderstorm cloud, small ice crystals blown upward collide with falling soft hail (graupel); each collision separates charge — the light crystals carry PLUS up to the top, the pellets carry MINUS down to the lower cloud. When enough charge builds, a faint stepped leader gropes down in short steps; near the ground an upward streamer rises from a tree top and they touch — and the BRIGHT flash we see (the return stroke) races from the GROUND UP to the cloud. The channel heats to ~28,000 °C (≈ 5× the sun's surface), the air bursts outward → thunder.
**Device:** the hero "Tia" (sketchbook terracotta doodle). It watches a storm from a hill (hook), floats up into the cloud (cloud), rides an ice crystal and hops onto a falling soft-hail pellet, picking up a plum "−" badge (collide) — it IS a negative charge now — sinks to the cloud base (charge), LEADS the faint leader down step by step (leader), meets the streamer (meet) → SLAM: the bolt blazes from the tree up; the hero has been knocked aside, its badge gone (strike). It sweats next to the hot channel (heat), gets blown back to the hill by the thunder (thunder). **The end is the start, changed:** same hill, same cloud, a distant flash — and now a big up-arrow along it; the hero points UP.

**Format:** F5 fixed-hero journey, morph-joined on the hero; one hard cut (SLAM = 34.5 s) into "strike" inside the pause after "meet". **Style:** sketchbook (bare plain page, graphite + ink, scribble colour, Pangolin lettering). 9:16, 1080×1920, 24 fps, 120 BPM, ≈ 60.5 s, Vietnamese VO (Tuan Khang, eleven_v3).

**Colour = one meaning:** TERRACOTTA = the hero only · BLUE `COL.pos/posL` = POSITIVE "+" and the ice crystals that carry it · PLUM `COL.neg/negL` = NEGATIVE "−" (soft-hail pellets, cloud base, the hero's badge, the leader's tip) · YELLOW `COL.flash` = LIGHT only (return stroke, flashes, flash-lit washes) · RED-ORANGE `COL.hot` = HEAT only · graphite = the faint leader, the unlit streamer, cloud washes, sound rings · ink = outlines, ground, tree.

## Claims + sources (research.md)
- Charge separation by graupel–ice-crystal collisions with supercooled water, crystals + up, graupel − in the middle/lower cloud — the widely accepted (non-inductive) mechanism (NSSL; still researched → script simplifies "âm dồn xuống đáy mây").
- Stepped leader ~50 m steps, faint; upward streamers from tall objects (NWS).
- The bright return stroke runs from the ground up to the cloud, ~1/3 c (NWS). Dart leaders → flicker (NWS).
- Channel ≈ 50,000 °F ≈ 28,000 °C ≈ 5× the sun's surface; rapid expansion → thunder (NWS lightning-science-thunder).
- Cumulonimbus tops commonly reach 10–15+ km (script: "cao hơn mười cây số").
