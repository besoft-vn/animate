# Brief — "Màn hình cảm ứng biết ngón tay bạn ở đâu?" (Vạn vật vận hành như thế nào, 21/11/2026)

**Idea (one line):** a phone screen does not measure how hard you press; under the glass is a transparent grid of row and column electrodes, every crossing a tiny capacitor whose field fringes up through the glass; the chip pulses each row and listens on each column; a finger (a conductor tied to your body) pulls part of that field away, so the readings at the crossings under it dip; the chip sweeps the whole grid ~120×/s and the centre of the dip is where you touch. A woollen glove (insulator) pulls nothing away → no dip → no touch.
**Device:** the hero is A FINGERTIP (`heroFinger` in props.js: skin capsule, nail, two dark eyes; its TIP is 70 px below its centre). It taps the phone, watches the glass lift off, watches the field, then touches and "steals" field lines into itself; the payoff crosshair lands on its tip. **The end is the start, changed:** the same phone, the hero touching it, but now the grid and the dip glow faintly under the glass.

**Format:** F5 fixed-hero journey, morph-joined on the hero; one hard cut (SLAM ≈ 43.0 s) into "locate" inside the pause after "sweep". **Style:** isometric (white ground, constant hairlines, white faces, springs; Pangolin text). 9:16, 1080×1920, 24 fps, 120 BPM grid, ≈ 61.5 s, Vietnamese VO (Tuan Khang, eleven_v3).

**Colour = one meaning:** BLUE `COL.field/fieldM/fieldL` = the electric field / the signal (field lines, the drive pulse, readings/bars) · SKIN `COL.skin*` = the finger/body (hero only) · DARK `PAL.accent` = the found touch point (`crosshair`) — only in locate (+ faint in end) · WOOL `COL.wool*` = the glove · grey hairlines = glass, electrodes, chip, everything else. A weaker reading = a SHORTER / PALER blue, never another colour.

## Claims + sources (research.md)
- Projected-capacitive (mutual) touch: ITO (or metal mesh) row/column electrodes, each crossing a capacitor, the controller drives TX rows and senses RX columns (Microchip, Infineon/Cypress app notes; Wikipedia "Touchscreen").
- A finger diverts part of the field to ground → mutual capacitance at nearby crossings DROPS (never say the finger "adds" capacitance).
- "lần lượt từng hàng" is a simplification (controllers may drive several rows with coded signals).
- Scan ~120 Hz is a common phone touch-sampling figure (Samsung press; range 20–240 Hz) → script says "chừng".
- Insulating gloves don't work; capacitive screens don't sense pressure (resistive ones did).
